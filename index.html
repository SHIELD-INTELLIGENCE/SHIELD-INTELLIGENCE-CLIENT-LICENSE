1. Purpose

This Client License governs the rights granted by SHIELD Intelligence (“SHIELD”) to a client (“Client”) in relation to websites, web applications, software, platforms, dashboards, digital systems, custom software, designs, and other digital products developed and delivered by SHIELD (“Client Product”).

This License is intended to establish clear ownership, usage, payment, maintenance, intellectual-property, and service rights for SHIELD client products.

A project-specific agreement, proposal, statement of work, quotation, or service agreement may contain additional commercial terms. Where expressly agreed in writing, those project-specific terms shall supplement this License.

2. Definitions

2.1 Client Product

“Client Product” means the specific finished digital product developed by SHIELD for the Client under an agreed project.

This may include, depending on the project:

  • source code
  • compiled code
  • website interfaces
  • web applications
  • application logic
  • databases and schemas
  • project-specific functionality
  • project-specific designs
  • project-specific UI implementation
  • documentation
  • configuration
  • integrations
  • assets specifically created for the Client

2.2 SHIELD Background IP

“SHIELD Background IP” means technology, intellectual property, systems, tools, frameworks, libraries, components, templates, methodologies, designs, security systems, encryption systems, development systems, deployment systems, code, concepts, processes, know-how, and other materials that:

  1. existed before the Client Product;
  2. were independently developed by SHIELD;
  3. are designed for reuse across multiple products or clients; or
  4. constitute SHIELD’s general engineering, design, security, or development infrastructure.

SHIELD Background IP remains the exclusive property of SHIELD.

2.3 Reusable Improvements

“Reusable Improvements” means improvements, modifications, abstractions, optimizations, components, systems, or techniques developed while creating a Client Product that are reasonably capable of being reused in SHIELD’s own products or other client projects without disclosing the Client’s confidential information or proprietary business logic.

Reusable Improvements remain SHIELD intellectual property.

3. Client Ownership

Upon:

  1. completion of the agreed project;
  2. full payment of all amounts due for the project; and
  3. activation of the applicable license,

the Client shall receive ownership of the Client-specific portions of the delivered Client Product.

This includes project-specific work created specifically for the Client and not constituting SHIELD Background IP or Reusable Improvements.

Client ownership may include:

  • custom project code;
  • custom business workflows;
  • project-specific interface implementation;
  • project-specific designs;
  • project-specific database structures;
  • custom functionality;
  • documentation created specifically for the Client.

The Client does not acquire SHIELD Background IP, Reusable Improvements, internal SHIELD systems, proprietary security technology, reusable frameworks, or SHIELD confidential technology merely because those technologies are incorporated into or used to develop the Client Product.

4. SHIELD Background IP

SHIELD retains all rights, title, and interest in SHIELD Background IP.

This includes, without limitation:

  • reusable code;
  • internal frameworks;
  • reusable components;
  • UI systems;
  • design systems;
  • templates;
  • development tooling;
  • deployment tooling;
  • internal APIs;
  • security architecture;
  • encryption systems;
  • authentication architecture;
  • proprietary algorithms;
  • reusable infrastructure;
  • internal automation;
  • AI-assisted development workflows;
  • engineering methodologies;
  • technical know-how;
  • general design methodologies.

Nothing in this License shall be interpreted as transferring ownership of SHIELD Background IP to the Client.

Where SHIELD Background IP is necessary for the Client to operate the Client Product, the Client receives the necessary right to use that component as part of the Client Product for the duration of the applicable license.

5. Reusable Technology

SHIELD may reuse general-purpose technology, components, engineering techniques, design systems, frameworks, concepts, and Reusable Improvements developed during a Client project.

SHIELD may use such technology:

  • in its own products;
  • in future SHIELD products;
  • in other client projects;
  • internally for research and development.

SHIELD shall not use or disclose the Client’s confidential information, proprietary business information, private data, trade secrets, or unique business logic merely by exercising this right.

6. Client Brand, Content and Materials

The Client retains ownership of its:

  • company name;
  • trademarks;
  • logos supplied by the Client;
  • product information;
  • photographs supplied by the Client;
  • written content supplied by the Client;
  • proprietary business information;
  • customer information;
  • confidential data;
  • other materials owned by the Client.

SHIELD receives the necessary permission to use such materials solely for the purpose of developing, operating, maintaining, demonstrating, or supporting the Client Product.

Client branding does not become SHIELD property.

However, SHIELD retains ownership of its own:

  • design systems;
  • reusable visual systems;
  • templates;
  • general layouts;
  • reusable UI components;
  • design methodology;
  • generic visual concepts.

A visual system developed by SHIELD may therefore be reused in other projects where doing so does not reproduce the Client’s protected branding, confidential material, or proprietary identity.

7. Third-Party Software and Services

Client Products may incorporate third-party software, open-source libraries, APIs, hosting providers, cloud infrastructure, payment providers, authentication services, analytics services, databases, CDN services, domain services, or other external technologies.

Such third-party components remain subject to their respective licenses and terms.

SHIELD does not transfer ownership of third-party intellectual property to the Client.

SHIELD may use open-source software where appropriate and in accordance with applicable open-source licenses.

8. Client Modification and Source-Code Transfer

The Client may not independently transfer, redistribute, resell, sublicense, or commercially repurpose the Client Product outside the scope of this License.

The Client may request a full source-code/repository handover by contacting SHIELD in writing.

Before such transfer:

  1. all outstanding subscription, maintenance, hosting, development, or other applicable fees must be paid;
  2. any outstanding contractual obligations must be satisfied;
  3. SHIELD may remove proprietary SHIELD Background IP that is not required to operate the Client Product;
  4. SHIELD may remove confidential security systems, encryption logic, internal infrastructure, proprietary tooling, credentials, secrets, and other SHIELD-protected technology;
  5. SHIELD will provide the transferable Client-specific project materials agreed for handover.

After the applicable handover is completed and all required fees are paid, the Client may independently modify the transferred Client-specific source code.

SHIELD Background IP remains SHIELD property even after source-code transfer.

9. No Unauthorized Resale or Sublicensing

The Client may not:

  • resell the Client Product;
  • sublicense the Client Product;
  • rent or lease the Client Product;
  • white-label the Client Product for unrelated third parties;
  • distribute copies to unrelated businesses;
  • use the Client Product as a commercial product for third parties;

unless SHIELD has provided written authorization and the applicable source-code/handover requirements have been completed.

The Client’s license is tied to the Client entity for which the product was developed.

Transfer of the Client Product to another legal entity requires SHIELD’s written authorization.

10. License Term

The Client’s usage rights begin only after:

  • the project has been fully paid for; and
  • the Client Product has been delivered or activated.

For subscription-based products, the license remains active while the applicable subscription and required payments remain current.

For products purchased under a lifetime license, the Client receives perpetual usage rights to the applicable Client Product, subject to this License and any continuing third-party service requirements.

11. Payment and Delivery

SHIELD is not required to deliver the completed Client Product, source code, credentials, deployment access, or other deliverables until all required amounts for the applicable delivery milestone have been paid.

Failure to complete payment may result in:

  • delayed delivery;
  • suspension of services;
  • suspension of hosted access;
  • withholding of source-code transfer;
  • termination of applicable services.

SHIELD will not be required to provide unpaid deliverables.

Before suspending an active service for payment default or a remediable breach, SHIELD will ordinarily provide written notice to the Client and reasonable opportunity to resolve the issue, unless immediate suspension is reasonably necessary to protect SHIELD, the Client, infrastructure, users, or security.

Once the applicable issue has been resolved and required payments have been received, SHIELD may restore the service where technically and commercially feasible.
